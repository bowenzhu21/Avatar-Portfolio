# Bay Area scenery

The `bay-area-*` videos and posters are adapted from **Sunrise at Golden Gate Bridge** by **KEHN HERMANO** on Pexels:

- Source: https://www.pexels.com/video/sunrise-at-golden-gate-bridge-6657004/
- License: https://www.pexels.com/license/ (checked November 25, 2026). Pexels permits free website use and modification; attribution is appreciated but not required. This use does not imply the creator's endorsement.
- Original: 3840 × 2160, 29.97 fps, approximately 36 seconds.
- Adaptation: source seconds 3–23, with a one-second loop dissolve; 24 fps H.264, no audio, fast-start MP4. The desktop version is 1920 × 1080; mobile uses a 720 × 1280 portrait crop that keeps the bridge tower in view.
- Posters are matching opening frames. The source master is not bundled.

The older `portfolio-bg*` and `background.jpg` assets predate this update and are no longer used for ambient scenery.
